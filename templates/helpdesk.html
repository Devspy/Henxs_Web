Frequently Asked Questions

View all
How do I reset my password?

Open your profile settings and choose Reset Password. If you cannot sign in, submit an Account & Access ticket.

Why is a camera showing as offline?

Check the camera power and network connection, then refresh Device Management. Create a Technical Support ticket if it remains offline.

How can I export an events report?

Open Analytics & Report, choose your date range and filters, then use the export option.

How do I update an assigned task?

Open Task Management, select the task, and update its status or assignment from the task details panel.
